Privacy Policy

Effective July 23, 2026 · applies to the Synk beta

Synk is currently in private beta. This policy will be updated before public launch; material changes will be announced in the app.

What we collect

Synk collects your email address, authentication credentials, profile details you provide (name, username, avatar), and the expense data you choose to enter — bills, receipts you scan, splits, and group membership. We use this information to provide the service and improve your experience.

How we use your data

Your data is stored securely and used only to operate Synk. We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties except as necessary to provide the service — payment processing is handled by Stripe, and receipt images you scan are processed to extract line items. Payment card details are handled directly by Stripe and never touch Synk's servers.

Your rights

You can access, export, and delete your data at any time. Visit Privacy Settings in the app to export your data or request deletion. Deletions are permanent and cannot be reversed.

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, data is removed within 30 days unless we are legally required to retain it.
