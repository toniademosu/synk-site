Support

A real person reads every message · updated August 9, 2026

The fastest way to get help is to email hello@synk.money. We aim to reply within two business days. Synk is in private beta, so if something is broken, telling us genuinely helps — send a screenshot if you can.

Reporting a bug

Email us with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot is worth more than a description. If the app showed an error message, send that text — it tells us exactly where to look.

Resetting your password

Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we will email you a reset link. If it has not arrived within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then email us and we will sort it out.

Exporting or deleting your data

Open Profile → Privacy Settings in the app. You can export everything Synk holds about you, or delete your account outright. Deletion is permanent and removes your data within 30 days.

A bill or a balance looks wrong

Synk calculates from what has been entered, so the usual cause is an amount, a split mode, or a participant that needs correcting. Open the bill and tap into the breakdown to see how each share was worked out. If the maths still looks wrong to you, send us the bill title and a screenshot — we would rather find a real bug than have you work around one.

Someone added me to a bill or a pool

You choose whether to accept. An invite stays pending until you answer it, and declining takes you out of the split. Nothing is taken from you for being invited.

Payments

During the beta Synk does not move money on your behalf. Balances are a record of who owes what, and you settle up between yourselves — mark it paid in the app once you have. When payment features are switched on, card and bank processing is handled by Stripe, and we will announce it in the app first.

Deleting the app

Removing Synk from your device does not delete your account. If you want your data gone, delete the account from Privacy Settings first.

Still stuck?

Email hello@synk.money and tell us what you need.
